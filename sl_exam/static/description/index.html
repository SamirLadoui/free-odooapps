School Exams

Plan It, Mark It, Publish It

Exams with mark sheets, letter grades and pass rates that work themselves out. Built around one idea: an unmarked paper is empty, not zero, and it should never look like a fail.

Key Features

Empty Is Not Zero

A paper nobody has marked yet has no grade, no percentage and no fail. It is simply left out of the averages until someone marks it.

Absent Is Not A Zero Score

A student who sat no paper is marked absent, and is excluded from the exam's average and from their own. Scoring nothing and sitting nothing are different facts.

Publishing Has To Be Earned

An exam cannot be published while any paper is unmarked. Publishing is telling students their result, and a half-marked sheet is not something to tell anyone.

Grades Follow The Marks

Enter a mark and the percentage, letter grade and pass or fail follow from the exam's own total and pass mark. Nothing is maintained by hand.

Statistics Per Exam

Pass rate, average and highest mark, computed over the papers actually marked rather than over everyone on the register.

Typos Refused At Entry

Marks above what the exam is worth or below zero are refused, and a pass mark above the total is refused outright since nobody could pass it.

Getting Started

  1. Install the module. It extends the School app.
  2. Open School > Exams and create one: class, subject, date, total and pass mark.
  3. Press Start Marking to load the enrolled students, then enter the marks.
  4. Press Publish once every paper is marked.

Good To Know

  • Grade bands are inclusive at the bottom: 80 percent is an A, 79.99 is a B.
  • Each student's Exam Results tab shows their history and an average across published exams only.
  • An exam dated outside its academic year is refused, as is a student from another class.
