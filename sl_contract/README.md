# Recurring Contracts

> Retainers And Subscriptions That Invoice Themselves

Set the lines and the recurrence once, and the invoices generate on schedule. Every invoice line states the exact service period it covers, so the customer can see what they are paying for.

## Features

- **Any Recurrence You Need** - Every N days, weeks, months or years, invoiced in advance for the period about to start or in arrears for the one just finished.
- **The Schedule Is Visible** - The next invoice date is a field you can see and correct, not a hidden calculation. It advances only when an invoice is actually created, so nothing drifts.
- **Lines With Their Own Dates** - A line can start later or stop earlier than the contract itself. Add a service in month four and it bills from month four; end one early and it drops off while the rest carries on.
- **Closes Itself When It Should** - Give a contract an end date and it invoices the final period, then closes and records why in its own log. No end date means it runs until you close it.
- **Periods On The Invoice** - Each invoice line carries the service period it covers, written out, so a customer questioning a charge can answer their own question.
- **Automatic Or On Demand** - A daily scheduled action invoices everything due, isolating each contract so one bad configuration cannot stop the rest. An Invoice Now button covers the times you do not want to wait.

## Getting Started

1. Install the module and go to Accounting > Customers > Contracts.
2. Create a contract, pick the customer, set the recurrence and add your lines.
3. Press Start, then Invoice Now once to confirm the output looks right.
4. Enable the Contracts: Generate Recurring Invoices scheduled action. It ships disabled.

## Good To Know

- Invoices are created in draft, so nothing is sent or posted without you.
- Each invoice is linked back to its contract, and the contract shows its invoice count.
- A contract with no lines cannot be started, and a period with no applicable line will not silently produce an empty invoice.
