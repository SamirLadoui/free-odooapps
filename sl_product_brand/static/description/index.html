Product Brands

A Brand Is Not A Product Category

Categories are how you account for a product; brands are who made it. Trying to express both in one hierarchy is where product data usually goes wrong. This gives brands their own place.

Key Features

Brands As Their Own Thing

A Brand field on every product, separate from the category, with its own filter and group-by in the product search so you can report on either.

Proper Brand Records

Each brand carries a logo, a short code, a website and an optional link to the manufacturer's contact record, with a kanban view that actually shows the logos.

Names And Codes Are Unique

The same brand cannot be typed in twice under two spellings, which is exactly how reporting quietly ends up split in half.

Straight Through To The Products

Every brand shows how many products carry it, with a button through to exactly those products.

History Stays Honest

A brand still used by products cannot be deleted, only archived, so historical orders keep meaning what they meant when they were placed.

Getting Started

  1. Install the module and open Sales > Configuration > Brands.
  2. Add your brands, with logos if you have them.
  3. Set the Brand field on your products.
  4. Filter or group the product list by brand.

Good To Know

  • Brand is stored on the product template and mirrored onto every variant, so it is available for filtering and reporting anywhere.
  • Sales managers maintain brands; everyone else can see them.
