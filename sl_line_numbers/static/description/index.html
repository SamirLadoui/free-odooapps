Invoice Line Numbers

Number the lines of an invoice, on screen and on paper

"The second line is wrong" is how people talk about an invoice on the phone, and Odoo gives them nothing to count with. The customer counts the lines one way, the supplier counts them another, and the call goes round in circles. This numbers every invoice and bill line, in the form and on the printed document alike, so both sides are looking at the same numbers.

Key Features

The same number on both

What the form shows and what the customer holds agree, because they come from the same place.

Headings are not numbered

Sections and notes are headings. Numbering them would mean the fifth line on paper is not line five, which is the confusion this is meant to end.

Renumbers as you reorder

The number is a position, worked out when it is shown. Move a line up and everything below it renumbers at once.

Nothing to repair

Because nothing is stored, there is no numbering to go stale after an import, a duplicate or a credit note.

Bills as well as invoices

Customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes and refunds all get the same treatment.

The column can be put away

It is an optional column in the lines list, so anybody who does not want it on screen can switch it off and still have it on the paper.

Getting Started

  1. Install the module.
  2. Open any invoice: the lines are numbered in a column at the front.
  3. Print or preview it, and the numbers are in front of each description.

Good To Know

  • Only the lines a customer sees are counted; tax and total lines are not.
  • The number appears in front of the description rather than as a separate printed column, so section and note rows cannot be knocked out of alignment.
  • Nothing about how Odoo orders or stores the lines is changed.
