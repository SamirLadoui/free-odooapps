# Product Packs

> Sell one product and put its contents on the order

The starter kit, the gift set, the hamper, the buy-the-whole-shelf bundle. Odoo can already do this with a manufacturing bill of materials marked as a kit, which means installing Manufacturing in order to sell a hamper. A pack here is simply a product with a list of what is in it.

## Features

- **Sold as one, picked as many** - Confirming the order puts the parts on it, so the warehouse picks things that exist and the customer's paperwork says what they are actually getting.
- **The quotation still reads right** - A quotation should say Starter Kit, not list six things. The parts arrive at confirmation, which is the moment the salesperson's view and the warehouse's view stop being the same.
- **Two ways to price one** - One price for the pack shows the parts at no charge. Priced line by line charges for each part and leaves the pack at nothing - what people expect when the bundle is a convenience rather than a discount.
- **A pack is not stock** - A pack cannot be a product you count, and the module refuses to let you make one. Counted, the order would reserve the pack and its contents at once and the same goods would leave twice.
- **Exploded once, not every time** - A pack line that already has its parts is left alone, so an order cancelled and confirmed again does not end up with the contents on it twice.
- **Quantities multiply** - Three of a pack containing two spoons is six spoons, on the order and on the delivery.

## Getting Started

1. Install the module.
2. Open a product that is a service or an uncounted product, tick Is A Pack, and list what is in it.
3. Choose whether the pack carries the price or the parts do.
4. Sell it. Confirm the order and the parts appear, ready to pick.

## Good To Know

- Packs do not go inside packs: a plain refusal is worth more than a cycle check nobody trusts.
- A pack with nothing in it is refused, because it would put nothing on the order.
- There is a button to explode the packs before confirming, for anyone who wants to see the parts first.
