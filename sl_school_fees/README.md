# School Fees

> What The Year Costs, Charged And Invoiced

Define your fee structures once, charge them to students, and raise real Odoo invoices against their contact records so the rest of Accounting just works.

## Features

- **Structures Per Year** - A structure belongs to an academic year and lists its items - tuition, books, transport - which add up to the total. Change it once and every future charge follows.
- **Empty Means Everyone** - Name the classes a structure applies to, or leave that blank and it covers every class in the year. Blank means everyone, not nobody, which is the reading people expect.
- **The Wrong Class Is Refused** - Charging a student a structure that does not apply to their class is rejected at entry. It is always a mistake, and the kind normally discovered months later.
- **Real Odoo Invoices** - A fee raises a normal customer invoice against the student's own contact record, so payments, reminders and statements all work with no extra setup.
- **Overdue That Tells The Truth** - A fee past its due date is flagged on the list and its form. Paying late clears it, because paying late is still paying, and so does cancelling.
- **Balance Per Student** - Every student shows their total charged and what is still outstanding, with cancelled fees excluded from both figures.

## Getting Started

1. Install the module. It extends the School app and needs Accounting.
2. Open School > Configuration > Fee Structures and define what the year costs.
3. Go to School > Fees, pick a student and a structure, and the amount fills in.
4. Press Create Invoice to bill it.

## Good To Know

- A student must be enrolled before they can be invoiced, since enrolment is what creates their contact record.
- The fee list opens filtered to what is still unpaid.
- Cancelled fees are excluded from a student's totals rather than deleted, so the history stays intact.
